What Viterbi actually does

1. Score every possible path by (product of emissions along it) × (product of transitions between its states).
2. A single left→right sweep keeps, for each epoch & state, the best score to reach it and a back-pointer — so it searches all 3N paths in O(N) time.
3. Backtrack from the best final state to get the one optimal sequence.

The magic: at epoch 7 the classifier may prefer NREM, but hopping REM→NREM→REM in adjacent epochs costs two rare-transition penalties — so staying in REM wins, and the dropped REM epoch is recovered.
